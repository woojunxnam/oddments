## Unresolved conflict: E30-116

The existing E30-116 question asks about individual reporting **under 247 CMR 20.03** and has answer **B** (14 calendar days for an arrest and formal disciplinary action). The text of [247 CMR 20.03(3)-(4)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-20-reporting/download) supports those literal 14-calendar-day obligations.

However, the same published Massachusetts regulatory code includes [247 CMR 10.03(1)(y)-(z)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-10-disciplinary-proceedings/download), which makes failure to report some disciplinary and governmental final actions **within seven business days** a potential ground for discipline. Subsection (aa) refers to criminal charges or convictions reported within **30 days**.

These deadlines might apply to differently defined triggering events, or multiple duties might apply to one event. The existing question's wording could wrongly teach that the §20.03 fourteen-day period is always the sole or most protective deadline. **The answer B is not adjudged incorrect simply for relying on §20.03; the question is withheld until the overlapping §10.03 issue is clarified or the question is explicitly narrowed.**

## Important law distinctions confirmed

- **Personnel:** [Policy 2026-02](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2026-02-ratios-supervision-and-dedicated-training-personnel/download) governs the new 1:4 staffing composition and the special exclusively-immunization four-intern pathway. [247 CMR 8](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-8-pharmacy-interns-and-technicians/download) distinguishes certified technician prescription intake and CII handling from general technician/trainee scope.
- **Renewal and education:** [247 CMR 4](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-4-personal-registration-renewal-continuing-education-requirement/download), [Policy 2020-01](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2020-01-pharmacist-license-reactivation-after-expiration-beyond-one-renewal-cycle-pdf/download) and [Policy 2021-04](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2021-04-continuing-education-ce-requirements-pdf/download) distinguish a >60-day lapse from >2-year lapse, and 5/3 annual specialty CE for even temporary sterile/complex nonsterile verification.
- **Oversight:** [247 CMR 9](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-9-professional-practice-standards/download) covers MOR absence, inspection, correction plans and pharmacist operational duties; [247 CMR 10](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-10-disciplinary-proceedings/download) covers investigatory cooperation, reciprocal discipline, deceptive CE submissions and the seven-day summary-action hearing.
- **Reports:** [247 CMR 20](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-20-reporting/download) separates 7-business-day serious dispensing / compounded adverse-event reporting, 14-calendar-day general reports and longer retention. [M.G.L. c.112 §65G](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXVI/Chapter112/Section65G) supports discretionary URAMP complaint treatment after admission.
- **Licenses:** [247 CMR 3](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-3-pharmacist-licensure-requirements/download) limits NABP's reciprocity approval to preliminary review. [247 CMR 6](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-6-licensure-of-pharmacies/download) requires nonresident pharmacy licenses for Massachusetts Schedule VI shipment, with separate compounding credentials. [247 CMR 14](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-14-petition-for-waiver/download) does not automatically extend a five-year waiver because a new application is pending.
