### Source conflict explicitly resolved
The original 2016 DPH Schedule II opioid circular retains outdated wording suggesting that all undispensed remainder becomes void. The updated 105 CMR 721.055(B) (Sept. 27, 2024) and M.G.L. c.94C §18(d¾) permit lawful remainder filling at the same pharmacy within applicable 30-day conditions. New case E30-109 tests ONLY the still-required original prescription notation and the DPH confirmation-and-documentation process, not superseded remainder language.
