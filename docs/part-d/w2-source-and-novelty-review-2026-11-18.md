## Existing-law duplication corrections BEFORE final W2 draft

The first drafts revealed several repeats. These were **replaced, not merely reworded**:

- **D022:** Original one-business-day environmental report receipt time repeated E30-160/E30-215. Replaced by Policy 2026-03 §III(G)'s **repeat action level in the same ISO area within 60 days, consecutive or nonconsecutive, requires microbiologist**.
- **D024:** Original nonviable mandatory sterile-compounding suspension was already tested in E30-160/C105. Replaced by **pass-through between ISO 7 and ISO 8 follows the cleaner-space action level**, and a provided result exceeds that limit.
- **D033:** Original missing DEA-number addition + change-patient-name repeated E30-199. Replaced by **NDC absent (not federally required) versus dosage form absent (required)** in a two-record Schedule IV Rx comparison.

**New FDA 2026 clinical product evidence** was deliberately included: O-desmethyltramadol's temporary Schedule I listing effective Aug. 12, 2026 (different from prescription tramadol Schedule IV); AUVELITY's 2026 FDA label identifies federal noncontrolled dextromethorphan/bupropion, which is Massachusetts Schedule VI as an Rx product; LIBRAX's exempt-listed chlordiazepoxide/clidinium product is distinguished from Schedule IV single-ingredient chlordiazepoxide; VIVITROL naltrexone is noncontrolled versus BRIXADI buprenorphine Schedule III with a restricted direct-provider REMS.

## Official-law research and version gates

### Board Policy 2026-03 (adopted 2026-09-03)

[Massachusetts Action Level Environmental Monitoring Policy](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2026-03-action-level-environmental-monitoring-results/download) §§I–VI governs sterile-compounding EM in ISO areas. D021 uses the literal `>` operator in its nonviable-air, viable-air and surface tables, preventing an incorrect equal-to-threshold action. D022 tests 60-day repeated excursions. D024 tests that a pass-through follows the ISO class of the **cleaner** adjoining area, not merely the room where staff stand. D023 uses the [Board Environmental Monitoring Advisory](https://www.mass.gov/doc/environmental-monitoring-pdf/download), revised 2026-09-03, to distinguish representative dynamic sampling from sampling immediately after a deep clean.

**Nuance:** This policy distinguishes conditional continuation after certain viable results from mandatory suspension for nonviable particle actions; earlier A/B and C already tested much of the latter, so the final W2 questions do not duplicate those conclusions. Source-retrieval limitation: the official PDF content was inspected through available extracted text. Image screenshot rendering of the mass.gov document returned HTTP 403, so no visual PDF page-verification success is claimed.

### USP <800> hazardous-drug handling

[Board Advisory: USP <800> in Community Pharmacies](https://www.mass.gov/doc/usp-800-in-community-pharmacies/download) (revised 2022-02-03) supports HD broken-dose/spill controls, permitted storage of intact finished antineoplastic or certain reproductive-risk drugs with pharmacy policy, at-least-annual job-related competency, and required containment of HD API compounding (including a negative-pressure environment). [247 CMR 9.01(3)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-9-professional-practice-standards/download) incorporates current USP chapters subject to Board rules.

**Limit:** The full licensed/current USP <800> chapter was not directly authenticated. Questions are narrowed to Board advisory propositions. Final exam-date review should verify supersession/updated USP where material; do not label an independently verified full chapter.

### Automated Non-Sterile Compounding (2025-11-06 Board memorandum)

[Official Board memorandum](https://www.mass.gov/doc/automated-non-sterile-compounding-devices-pdf/download) has three separate requirements: FDA approval/need or documentation of manufacturer validation; ingredient/API/approved-IND compatibility; and device cleanability to minimize cross-contamination. Crucially, automation does NOT relieve pharmacy responsibility for preparation accuracy and quality. D029–D032 test **four different decisions**; no unverified claim that FDA clearance is universally required.

D032 documentation is additionally corroborated by [Board Retail Inspection Template items 92–93](https://www.mass.gov/doc/retail-pharmacy-inspection-template/download) (MFR and CR for each nonsterile preparation) and [Policy 2023-07 §VI](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2023-07-non-sterile-compounding-pdf/download). The entire current USP <795> original normative text was not directly accessed.

### Prescription record interpretation and current controlled schedules

[Board/DCP Policy 2018-01](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2018-01-permitted-prescription-changes-and-additions-pdf/download), revised 2024-01-11, controls authorized changes including change to patient/prescriber identity and patient address. Federal [21 CFR 1306.05(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1306/section-1306.05) lists essential controlled-prescription fields (NOT NDC). [21 CFR 1311.170(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1311/section-1311.170) renders an EPCS invalid when required prescription content is lost during transmission, while preserving harmless software-field format conversion.
