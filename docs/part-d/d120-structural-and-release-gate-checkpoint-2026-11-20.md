## Legal/source watch list

1. **D069 and D072 — Board/DEA reporting:** Massachusetts Board [Policy 2026-01](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2026-01-loss-or-theft-of-controlled-substances-pdf/download) applies to resident licensees and requires Board RLCS and DEA Form 106 **if applicable** within seven calendar days; [21 CFR 1301.76(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1301/section-1301.76) separately requires initial DEA notice within one business day and Form 106 within 45 days. Maintain actor, discovery vs confirmation, form submission, and jurisdiction distinctions.
2. **D071 and D088 — hospital unit stock and suspected loss:** the state [hospital/clinic controlled-substances accountability guidelines](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/general-accountability-guidelines-for-controlled-substances-in-hospitals-and-clinics-with-pharmacies) describe immediate Drug Control Program reporting for suspected or confirmed loss and two-licensed-person waste rules. Keep this distinct from the resident retail Board RLCS process.
3. **D089 and D090 — Manager of Record timing:** [247 CMR 9.23(2)(f)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-9-professional-practice-standards/download) continues to display a *ten-day personal resignation notice*, while later [247 CMR 20.05(1),(3)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-20-reporting/download) and the Board's [2025 information for MORs](https://www.mass.gov/doc/information-for-managers-of-record-pdf/download) distinguish 14-calendar-day pharmacy and personal notices. **Explicitly reconcile the two operative-text provisions before freezing D090 or presenting an unqualified single clock.** D089's one-retail-pharmacy and 30-hour rule is textually present in 247 CMR 9.23(3)–(4).
4. **D118 — event-driven self-inspection:** 247 CMR 9.19(21) provides seven days after listed changes plus at least annual review and two-year record retention. Current PDF inspected.
5. **D101–D104 — breach notices:** check each individual/HHS/substitute/state reporting trigger and counting rule against the current 45 CFR 164 and M.G.L. c.93H, especially the different reporting clocks and affected-person thresholds.
