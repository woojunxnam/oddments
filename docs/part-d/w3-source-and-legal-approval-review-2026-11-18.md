### Special legal-source/version cautions

1. **DEA March 20, 2026 amendment:** Current §1305.05(d)/(e) and §1305.12(d) require a proper corporate grantor and distinguish registration application signers from officers. The correct Federal Register source is <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-03-20/pdf/2026-05482.pdf>. Previous alternate PDF identifier `2026-05525` was corrected. Some older DEA FAQs/examples reflect superseded text; operative CFR controls.
2. **MassHealth returns:** A superseded 2002 Pharmacy Manual reference associates `130 CMR 406.446` with returns. Current 2026 rule `406.446` concerns DUR/counseling; `130 CMR 456.621` separately governs eligible nursing-facility unit-dose returns. The question specifies Appendix F eligibility and full MassHealth payment rather than claiming any drug may be returned.
3. **FDA July 2020 repackaging guidance:** Only the conditional **commercial FDA-registered repackager** scope is tested. The shorter-of-six-months-or-25%-of-remaining-life measure is FDA's nonbinding enforcement-discretion policy, *not* a universal Massachusetts repackaging or compounding BUD.
4. **FDA Medication Guides and PPIs:** Under 21 CFR 208.24 and 310.501, format and setting-specific exceptions matter. Electronic Medication Guides requested by patients should not be generalized to missing medication information.
5. **DPH nonvaccine administration:** DCP 26-03-124 distinguishes recommended prescriber communication within 24 hours for nonserious reactions from immediate notice for serious life-threatening events, and separate conditional 247 CMR20.02 Board reporting.
6. **FDA data change risk:** Orange Book / Purple Book entries and authorized-generics list are date-sensitive. Confirm product codes and no-substitution rules at actual examination date.
