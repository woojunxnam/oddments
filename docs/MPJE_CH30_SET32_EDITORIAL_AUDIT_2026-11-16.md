## Precision notes and uncertainty handling

- **E30-156** — Mass.gov 247 CMR 4.00 current published 2017 regulation. Section 4.02(3) disallows ALL practice with expired registration; subsection (4) is discretionary Board condition upon >60-day lapse, not an automatic grace period or universal exam retest. A >2-year lapse follows separate §4.02(5).
- **E30-157** — 45 CFR 164.510(b)(1)–(3), eCFR current Nov. 13, 2026; HHS OCR family/friend guidance (last reviewed Dec. 28, 2022). Express objection by capable patient is respected under this authority; when patient absent, pharmacy may infer authorized pickup from context with professional judgment; incapacitated patient best-interest sharing limited to relevant PHI. No Part 2 or stricter state rule assumed.
- **E30-158** — 21 CFR 7.3(m) Class I hazard definition and FDA Enforcement Report FAQ: FDA may classify a firm-initiated voluntary recall AFTER it begins. Probability of harm, not documented injuries, determines hazard category. Draft does NOT claim FDA has formally classified the event and does not purport to issue a federal immediate-quarantine directive absent statutory grounds. Local segregation is standard product-integrity practice.
- **E30-159** — eCFR 21 CFR 1308.13(c)(11) perampanel III; 1308.14(c)(12) clobazam IV; 1308.15(e)(1) brivaracetam V, up to date as of 2026-11-13 (Title 21 last amended Nov. 12); 105 CMR 700.002(C)–(E) adopts those schedules. Drug-specific listings govern, not antiseizure class as a whole.
- **E30-160** — Massachusetts Board Policy 2026-03 was adopted Sept. 3, 2026 and expressly supersedes Policy 2023-09. ISO 7 viable air action level is >10 CFU/m3, so 11 qualifies. Board notification within 1 business day of microbiology report; nonviable particle results require suspension/recertification, unlike viable-results conditional risk mitigated ISO 7 buffer compounding capped at 24h room temperature or 4d refrigerated. Distinguish this Board policy from the informational Sept. 2026 Environmental Monitoring advisory and official USP <797> compendial text.
