## Important legal constraints, effective dates and disagreements

- **CDTM:** 247 CMR 16.03(5)(a) limits retail/community CDTM to patients aged 18 or older; written guardian consent is NOT a substitute. The age requirement is independently cumulative with written individual physician referral and Schedule VI-only retail CDTM prescribing. The source text is from the Dec. 15, 2017 regulation still used for this topic.
- **DSCSA:** The cited federal core duties to use authorized trading partners and maintain tracing information/records at least six years are separate from the enhanced exchange requirements addressed by FDA small-dispenser exemptions. This case explicitly excludes waivers/exemptions and tests written third-party custody agreements that do not relieve legal responsibility, avoiding the Nov. 27, 2026 exemption transition.
- **USP <800>:** USP's January 21, 2026 FAQs are technical support, not official interpretations. Q80 forbids pneumatic tubes for LIQUID HDs or NIOSH Table 1 antineoplastics; Q85 forbids automated counting/packaging of Table 1 antineoplastic solid units but permits eligible non-antineoplastics subject to case-specific assessment and SOPs. FAQ 5 reconciles historic <800> low/medium risk wording with current <797> Category 1/2/3; only Category 1 hazardous CSPs qualify for C-SCA.
- **Methadone:** DEA's Aug. 2026 fact sheet and current 21 CFR 1306.04/1306.07 distinguish appropriately registered analgesic prescribing from methadone maintenance/detoxification for OUD through certified OTPs. The restricted emergency three-day DISPENSING bridge is not a prescription for routine outpatient methadone maintenance. This question does not deny patient access to lawful pain treatment.
- **Compounding and packaging:** 247 CMR 9.01(15) imposes a precise customary simple/moderate NONSTERILE compounded medication availability duty, subject to extenuating circumstances/Board waiver, not indiscriminate complex sterile compounding. 247 CMR 9.08 controls multi-drug-single-dose packaging with maximum 60-day quantity, no CII/III or PRN doses, and tightly restricted same-patient return/repackaging.
- **Drug-specific schedules:** Ramelteon/Rozerem and pitolisant/Wakix are federal UNSCHEDULED Rx-only drugs, classified as Massachusetts Schedule VI, while suvorexant/daridorexant/solriamfetol/modafinil/carisoprodol have specific federal Schedule IV listings. Do not assume class-wide control; verify exact products and dosage forms.
- **Medication Guides:** Inpatient professional administration distribution is addressed in FDA's nonbinding enforcement-discretion guidance, distinct from binding 21 CFR 208.24(e)'s direct outpatient distribution requirements and separate REMS-specific conditions. The answer never claims a formal regulatory repeal.
- **Quality and professional discipline:** 247 CMR 10.03(1)(m) and (q) identify knowingly enabling unlicensed practice and failing without cause to cooperate with Board investigations as independent potential grounds, distinct from the serious-injury or reciprocal-license cases. 247 CMR 9.01(11),(14) separately prohibits remunerative facility referrals and issuing pharmacy-identifying blank prescription forms.
