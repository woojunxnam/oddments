## Duplication and ambiguity improvements

A cross-bank screen examined all **480 A–D** existing items and **E001–E040** against every E041–E060 question stem and its specific legal decision. No exact duplicate stems/IDs were found. The presence of nearby questions testing the same broad subject is **not** a true novelty pass unless the material condition differs; each question stores `closest_existing_ids`, `material_novelty` and `counterfactual_flip`.

- **E053 was replaced.** The first version asked for Schedule VI refill-history fields already tested in E013 for federal controlled refills. The new version focuses on the **prescriber-office employee as an authorized oral prescription communicator**, expressly assuming a valid Massachusetts electronic-prescribing exception and preserving the difference between the prescriber and the messenger.
- **E045** checks a distinct stimulant/nonstimulant drug family using brand/generic names not present in the existing 480 items.
- **E046** distinguishes newly tested **Nucynta/tapentadol Schedule II** from **Ultram/tramadol Schedule IV** instead of repeating another hydrocodone/tramadol-only drug classification.
- **E052** tests the *institutional sterile compounding pharmacy-specific* 9.04 exemptions without implying all controlled-substance statutes are waived.
- **E054–E056** each evaluate **different** provisions of 9.08: ahead-of-Rx single-drug prepackaging, prohibited PRN multi-drug pack content and post-return disposition of removed medication, respectively.

**Source quality note:** This is an AI-authored, officially sourced editorial review and automated schema/rationale check, not a qualified independent human pharmacy-law signoff and not an assessment using secure NABP exam content. Legal rules requiring an inaccessible normative USP text should continue to be placed on HOLD, rather than certified from an FAQ. No external human legal approval is claimed.
