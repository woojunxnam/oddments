## Independent law reading performed
The editor compared the new items with official [NABP pre-2027 Competency Statements](https://nabp.pharmacy/programs/examinations/mpje/competency-statements/) and operative Massachusetts [247 CMR 8.00](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-8-pharmacy-interns-and-technicians/download) and [247 CMR 9.00](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-9-professional-practice-standards/download). Each new item embeds its targeted subsection, official URL, five rationales, nearest old items, material novelty reason and a counterfactual that changes the outcome. This is **an AI editorial primary-source review**, not a human pharmacy-law attorney/pharmacist legal certification, calibrated testing, or comprehensive cross-regulation legal audit.

Key sourced microdecisions:
- 247 CMR **8.01(3),(10)**: supervised pharmacy intern may continue after approved graduation until pharmacist licensure.
- 247 CMR **8.07**: technician state licensure renews on birthday every two years; national certification does not replace it.
- 247 CMR **8.06(1)**: employee listing and written delegated duties/scope retrievable on Board request.
- 247 CMR **8.08**: qualified legally protected healthcare activity licensing exception.
- 247 CMR **9.18(6), 9.03**: counseling privacy/access and nondeceptive advertising.
- 247 CMR **9.06(2)–(5)**: high-overdose area continuous antagonist supply, nearest available referral, rescue kit counseling/pamphlet/label/expiry.
- 247 CMR **9.01(13)**: hospital pharmacy employee/household family exception.
- 247 CMR **9.18(10)**: inpatient pharmacist counseling exception (scope restricted to practice setting).
- 247 CMR **9.04(11),(3),(7)**: out-of-premises processing verification, emergency handwritten labeling and no-NDC manufacturer fallback.
- 247 CMR **9.07**, **9.08(1)(h)**: prior-dispensed medications in reusable planners and oral-liquid single-dose multi-product compounding exception.
- 247 CMR **9.05(3)(b)**: lower-cost brand-for-brand interchange labeling.
- 247 CMR **9.19(1),(8),(9),(14)**: references, license/registration display, onsite clinical/POA/CDTM documentation, posted pharmacy hours and advertisements.

- **E025 draft:** proposed pharmacy-referencing blank prescription forms duplicated existing **E30-173**. Replaced with physical private counseling-area accessibility and audiovisual privacy.
