However, the original **September 2026 Massachusetts pharmacist PEP DOCX** was inaccessible. Before full approval, check the exact pharmacist-specific minor eligibility, 50 kg threshold, baseline HIV test language, quantity limit, zero-refill rule and consent exceptions against that original guideline. Do not infer these details solely from CDC general advice.

**E30-130 — USP <795> BUD Table 4:** USP's public revision Open Forum table states **14-day refrigerated nonpreserved aqueous**, **35-day preserved aqueous at controlled room temp/refrigerated**, **90-day nonaqueous oral liquid**, **180-day other nonaqueous**, agreeing with key **A/C**. But the public 2022 educational slide is not the authenticated current operative USP–NF chapter. Obtain direct current compendial text / official updated table before mock approval. A confirmed numeric match in old slides is evidence, not full legal authority.
