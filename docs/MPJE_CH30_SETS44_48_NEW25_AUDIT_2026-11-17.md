## Corrections after first draft / independent nearest-neighbor screening

- **E30-231:** Initial PharmD residency-intern draft repeated E30-076 (PGY1 resident licensure). Replaced with a genuinely distinct **pharmacist graduation-calendar-year CE exemption under 247 CMR 4.03(8)** and no later-year carryover.
- **E30-236:** Initial expired certified-tech-status draft repeated E30-121's national certification lapse. Replaced with **the employing pharmacy's current-license verification duty on the day work commences** under 247 CMR 9.19(18).
- **E30-219:** Originally an additional Schedule V drug-name recognition exercise. Replaced with the **16 CFR 1700.14(a)(10) product-specific PPPA child-resistant packaging exceptions**, not the patient waiver covered in E30-029.
- **E30-237:** Originally an interrupted PrEP protocol situation; source for the granular state protocol was not sufficiently accessible, so replaced with the **14-day or smallest-unit-of-use Massachusetts Schedule VI continuity supply** under 247 CMR 9.04(12), differentiating ordinary refills/expired prescriptions.
- **E30-227:** Focuses only on the operative *42 CFR 2.24 intermediary list right* (written request, 3-year window, response 30 days), not the broader §2.25 accounting compliance-date uncertainty.
- **E30-233:** Focuses on the **federal Part 2 program breach notification** for fewer than 500 individuals, distinct from E30-033 (HIPAA 500-person threshold) and E30-073 (encryption/risk assessment).
- **E30-228:** Current active [Lenalidomide REMS Pharmacy Guide](https://www.lenalidomiderems.com/media/wp-content/uploads/LEN_Pharmacy_Guide.pdf) explicitly sets **24-hour** confirmation validity. This is not another isotretinoin or esketamine REMS window question.
- **E30-225:** Tests the Board's repeat environmental sampling and clearing action levels to restore ordinary BUD, not the initial ISO 7/ISO 5 adverse finding notifications E30-160 and E30-215.

## Legal-source verification and qualification

Primary authorities were reviewed for the key rules (Massachusetts statutory/case authority, official 247 CMR, Board policies, 21 CFR, 42 CFR, 45 CFR, 16 CFR, HHS OCR, and active FDA REMS program materials); the audit records each question's current public citation and separately reasoned correctness of all four choices.

**Important limitations:**
- E30-216's *Correa v. Schoeck*, 479 Mass. 686, recognizes a **limited PA-notice civil duty**; it neither guarantees insurance approval nor establishes automatic negligence liability from an insurance denial. Do not conflate with disciplinary sanction.
- E30-217 expressly assumes a pharmacy covered by HHS civil-rights language-access law; do not apply its funding condition universally without checking coverage.
- E30-225 cites the Massachusetts Board's **Policy 2026-03** for repeat environmental monitoring, with no pretense that the complete subscription-only USP–NF text was directly opened. The 10 existing FAQ-limited USP cases likewise retain prior source limitations.
- E30-230 uses **published DPH hospital/clinic controlled-substance accountability guidance**, not a newly promulgated regulation with an invented enforcement penalty.
- E30-237 uses **14 days**, not an obsolete 30-day emergency-supply figure from an older secondary inspection resource. See current [247 CMR 9.04(12)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-9-professional-practice-standards/download).
- E30-239 invokes a **PMP-reportable additional drug** (gabapentin) at pickup; this is distinct from federally controlled Schedule II and only allows a documented serious-hardship substitute for government ID under [247 CMR 9.04(14)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-9-professional-practice-standards/download).
- E30-240 tests the state's specific written pharmacy downtime and DUR SOP, **not** an invented cyber incident regulator-notification deadline.
- Candidate self-reports are only used for format/skill signals, never copied as questions or treated as verified legal requirements.
