## Caveats and unresolved items

- **288 A/B/C items remain without independent current-primary-source review.** They must remain labeled `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_PRIMARY_SOURCE_REVIEW`, rather than silently marked approved.
- Some reviewed questions have an **explicit scope/source caveat** rather than an unqualified legal endorsement, including D049–D050 (older accessible MassHealth return regulation with current consolidation not independently established), D048 (FDA commercial-repackager guidance vs general retail pharmacy legal duty), and E30-088 / E30-192 (specific Massachusetts PrEP/PEP statewide DOCX protocol details not fully authenticated).
- Some sterile/hazardous-compounding items rest on public Board guidance without authenticated access to the complete paid normative USP text. Review status must continue to expose this limitation.
- E30-075 presents a **December 1, 2026 hypothetical** based on the FDA exemption effective **November 27, 2026**. The scenario is forward-looking as of the review date.
- **Zero exact duplicates** is not a semantic-similarity or blueprint-coverage guarantee, and practice-bank passing scores are not a validated prediction of actual MPJE pass probability.

## Form E start decision

**Pre-Form E minimum quality gate: PASS WITH DOCUMENTED LEGAL-AUDIT DEBT.**

Start designing Form E **without waiting for an exhaustive re-audit of every A/B/C question**, but do not describe A–D as `fully legally approved` or use the 288 pending items as definitive evidence of readiness.

Each new Form E item must pass the following **before publication**:
1. Independent verification of **operative Massachusetts + federal primary legal sources**, correct as-of date, exceptions, and numerical thresholds.
2. Direct reasoning for **every answer alternative** (correct and incorrect), including why plausible distractors are legally wrong.
3. Stem and options checked for missing facts, misleading universal language, jurisdiction differences, ambiguity, and future effective-date errors.
4. Duplication and blueprint gap check against all 480 A/B/C/D items.
5. No deployment of a changed question or key unless compatible with historical exam grading and revisions.

Finish pending A/B/C review in parallel with Form E creation, prioritized by high-consequence controlled-substance and prescribing authority rules, USP <797>/<800>, prescription delivery/storage, licensing/discipline, current 2026 changes, and numerical/datetime exceptions.
