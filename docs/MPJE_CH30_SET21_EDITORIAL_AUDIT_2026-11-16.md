## Important scope distinctions

- Emergency pharmacist license suspension under 247 CMR 10.07 is a temporary action **before the final merits hearing**, with a separate limited necessity hearing within seven days. It is distinct from out-of-state reciprocal discipline and an advisory letter (E30-096).
- Dentist-controlled prescription authority is restricted to legitimate dental indications. A nonemergency Schedule II order to immediate family and any Schedule II–IV self-prescribing are prohibited by 234 CMR 5.06; ordinary drug-control and opioid rules still apply.
- Medication Guide duties remain in 21 CFR Part 208. The FDA Patient Medication Information (PMI) reform was on the **proposed/final-rule agenda in 2026**, not an effective replacement in the cited November 2026 source. Dispenser duties, physician withholding direction and explicit patient request are separate legal conditions. Official eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-208/subpart-B/section-208.26
- FDA-approved dronabinol in a sesame-oil soft-gel capsule is Schedule III; FDA-approved dronabinol oral solution is Schedule II. These product-specific listings are current in the DEA controlled-substances list dated Nov. 12, 2026 and incorporated by Massachusetts 105 CMR 700.002(B)–(C). Official state schedule: https://www.mass.gov/regulations/105-CMR-70000-implementation-of-mgl-c94c
- USP <800> FAQ dated January 21, 2026 is **technical support, not an official interpretation** of the compendial chapter. FAQ 68–71 restates mandatory receiving controls and clearly distinguishes nonmandatory impervious-plastic supplier packaging recommendation. The unmodified primary chapter's applicable receiving controls should be checked when facility policy is updated.
