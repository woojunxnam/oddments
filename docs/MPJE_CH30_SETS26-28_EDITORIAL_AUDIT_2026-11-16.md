## Legal caution / potential confusion addressed
1. **MOR:** Planned absence requires an appointed interim MOR **before** the absence, unlike the five-day window for unplanned absence. Dual retail MOR prohibited under 247 CMR 9.23; unrelated prior E30-051 covered unexpected absence.
2. **Return vs return-to-stock:** Drugs already dispensed to patients in error or suspected to be defective must be accepted for quarantine/disposal under 9.01(7); undelivered return-to-stock has separate 9.13 rules.
3. **Schedule classification:** October 2014 hydrocodone combination rescheduling to II, approved Xyrem III versus GHB I, lacosamide V, ketamine III and zolpidem IV; 105 CMR 700.002 uses corresponding federal schedules.
4. **USP:** Official revised <795> BUDs are 14/35/90/180 by preservative, water activity, and form; <800> assessment of risk cannot waive full protection for hazardous APIs or manipulation of NIOSH Table 1 antineoplastics. USP technical FAQs are informational, not formal USP interpretations.
5. **Patient rights:** A trained staff member may offer pharmacist counseling but cannot replace it; bedside discharge delivery requires consent, freedom of pharmacy choice, and direct handoff.
6. **Reporting/inspections:** Individual profile/name disclosures = 14 CALENDAR days; POC = 21 days unless Board directs otherwise and resubmission seven days after rejection; Board inspections may be unannounced. Deadlines were not extrapolated from unrelated provisions.
