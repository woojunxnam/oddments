### D090 reporting nuance — do not hide conflict

- 247 CMR 20.05(1) **pharmacy** must report change of MOR within 14 calendar days.
- 247 CMR 20.05(3) says **MOR individual** must report resignation or termination within 14 calendar days.
- 247 CMR 9.23(2)(f) separately retains **10 days for individual MOR** notification. Both appear as operative 2024 texts. D090 ONLY asks about the pharmacy duty and retains answer A, but the overlap requires Board interpretation / editorial caution before teaching an unqualified personal deadline.
