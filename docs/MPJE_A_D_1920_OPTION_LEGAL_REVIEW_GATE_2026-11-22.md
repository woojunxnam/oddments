## Corrections and holds that matter for MPJE

### 1. Actual E30-190 answer explanation error corrected in question bank

A/B `E30-190` (Category 2 nonhazardous sterile CSP) had inconsistent author metadata:
- Original **citation and note** already correctly referenced USP **<797> Table 13**.
- Original correct-option **C explanation** mistakenly referred to **Table 12**, which the Massachusetts Board sterile inspection tool identifies as **Category 1**, not Category 2.
- **One explanation-only string corrected to Table 13** in the original `questions.js`; its **stem, options, scoring key, ID and all other 239 question objects are unchanged**. This is not a new validated norm-text signoff.
- Official Massachusetts [Sterile Compounding Inspection Template, item 73.4](https://www.mass.gov/doc/sterile-compounding-pharmacy-inspection-template-pdf/download): Category 2 CSP aseptically made from sterile starting components without sterility testing: **4 days controlled room temperature / 10 days refrigerated / 45 days freezer**, assuming chemical and physical stability. Table 12 is Category 1. These figures support the correct item but current full subscribed USP <797> chapter text remains unverified.

### 2. HOLD: E30-058 Massachusetts oral follow-up eRx clock

- [M.G.L. c.94C §20(c)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXV/Chapter94C/Section20) says oral Schedule II–V controlled Rx follow-up electronic transmission **within 2 days**, with written exception **within 7 days** (or shorter federal limit).
- [105 CMR 721.070(A)–(C)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/105-cmr-721-standards-for-prescription-format-and-security-in-massachusetts/download) says oral under ePrescribing exception followed by written/electronic prescription **within seven business days**, unless shorter federal law. [DPH Circular DCP19-12-108](https://www.mass.gov/circular-letter/circular-dcp-19-12-108-electronic-prescribing-and-dispensing-manual) echoes seven business days.
- The question intentionally avoids committing to a universal deadline; its existing answer set has not been demonstrated wrong, **but final legal signoff remains HOLD** until authority/agency reconciliation. Teach neither clock as a blanket rule.

### 3. HOLD: E30-342 Category 2 BUD after hood relocation

- Moving an ISO 5 PEC from classified cleanroom into unclassified SCA **does not automatically retain Category 2 BUD**; official USP FAQ and MA Board sterile inspection guidance corroborate direction.
- The exact directly applicable, current **normative USP <797>** clauses were not accessed in their entirety; **source hold remains open** rather than mislabeling a FAQ as full standard.

### 4. D090 MOR reporting: narrow question supported; individual personal clock conflict remains

- Pharmacy reports MOR change within **14 calendar days**: [247 CMR 20.05(1)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-20-reporting/download).
- MOR person reports within **14 calendar days** under [247 CMR 20.05(3)](https://www.mass.gov/doc/247-cmr-20-reporting/download), also stated in the Board's [2026 reporting overview](https://www.mass.gov/doc/reporting-requirement-overview-pdf/download).
- **247 CMR 9.23(2)(f)** still states **within ten days** for the individual MOR's resignation/termination notification. The Board's [Information for Managers of Record](https://www.mass.gov/doc/information-for-managers-of-record-pdf/download) favors 14 calendar days under 20.05 but does not remove the published text discrepancy.
- **D090's pharmacy-only key is supported**, but do not create an exam question assuming the MOR personal deadline is unambiguous.
