## Legal risk and effective-date notes

- **206:** NABP is the preliminary review agent, with the Board's final decision controlling; 247 CMR 3.02(1)(c) gives one year after NABP approval. Not permission to practice pending MA license issuance.
- **207:** State Circular DCP 26-08-125 effective Aug. 25, 2026; prior prescription cannot override new aura migraine. CDC's 2024 U.S. MEC (current referenced table) grades CHC migraine with aura Category 4. No assumption that all other hormonal methods are disallowed.
- **208:** Board's January 9, 2025 Veterinary Prescriptions guidance confirms no human-health NPI for veterinarians and no DEA number for Schedule VI; state MCSR is the pertinent identifier.
- **209:** Explicitly **hypothetical federal nonprescription-eligible product**; NO allegation that a named currently marketed codeine NDC is FDA OTC. Federal 21 CFR 1306.26(f) only applies if no other law requires prescription. Massachusetts §17(c) here requires one; emergency/other recognized exceptions excluded.
- **210:** Electronic CSOS order substitutes for paper DEA 222, not for individual digital certificate custody or the purchaser's quantity/date receipt record electronically linked to the original, 21 CFR 1305.22(g).
- **211:** 247 CMR 14.01(5)(b)–(d): post-June-2013 waivers expire fifth anniversary; a subsequent petition does not automatically extend previous approval.
- **212:** M.G.L. c94C §18(c) mandates independent telephone-or-other validation on SIII–V from outstate prescribers; gabapentin is still reportable to MassPAT although it is MA Schedule VI. Connecticut examination conducted in CT avoids uncertain telehealth jurisdiction assumptions.
- **213:** March 11, 2026 Circular DCP 26-03-124 permits pharmacist administration of listed IM cabotegravir under valid order for eligible adults, with training/CPR/documentation; Aug. 25, 2026 prescribing circular and oral-PrEP guideline do not by themselves establish unrestricted independent injectable prescribing.
- **214:** DEA §1308.15 names cenobamate Schedule V. Levetiracetam is federal noncontrolled/MA Schedule VI. MA 247 CMR 9.04(12) vs (13) one-year vs six-month Rx rules. MassPAT SII–V plus designated gabapentin, not all SVI.
- **215:** Board Policy 2026-03 adopted Sept. 3, 2026 (supersedes 2023-09). An 18-CFU ISO5 viable-air result crosses >1 action level and >15 significant-loss criterion; §II one-business-day Board notification and §III(C) within-BUD recall, prescriber contact, microbiologist, adverse-event surveillance. Unaffected PECs judged on risk, not necessarily universal shutdown.
