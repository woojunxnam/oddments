# Massachusetts MPJE — E/F/G Development after C/D Legal Review

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Status:** design only — NO E/F/G questions authored, published, or counted. User priority: accurate and practical law learning, not changing four-choice style or equalizing choice lengths. Keep original A/B/C/D question IDs, answer histories and explanations.

## Readiness criteria and scope

- A/B/C/D existing 480 items are a substantial learning bank but not a psychometrically calibrated NABP exam.
- Source-first legal review and correction of C/D are higher priority than new items. Do **not** regard a final question count as equivalent to final source signoff.
- If examination is **before 2027-03-01**, author to official 2026 NABP Competency Statements with legacy Areas 1–4. From **2027-03-01** use the new Content Outline Domains (30/30/20/20), which cannot be inferred merely by relabeling 2026 Areas.
- All future questions will maintain **four choices A–D**, individual why explanations, primary-law URLs, dates, actors, numerical thresholds and realistic state-versus-federal exceptions. Do not spend review time on superficial choice-length parity unless ambiguity affects the legal outcome.
- New question IDs and storage/manifest signatures must be versioned. E/F/G require separate mock sessions and 120/150 functionality, reuse the existing correct-answer review UI rather than duplicating logic.

## Form E — 120, foundational high-yield recall plus real decisions

Focus areas with limited **direct recognition and quick retrieval** in prior A/B/C/D:
- Federal / Massachusetts Schedule I–VI classification, names, brand/generic, *formulation-specific* exemptions, exempt compounds and MassPAT reporting
- Which prescriptions are valid: dentist/PA/NP/optometrist/podiatrist/veterinarian authority, hospital resident DEA suffix, nonresident prescribers
- Controlled Schedule II/III–V issue-date validity, day supply, emergency oral, partial filling, EPCS and Rx changes
- Pharmacist vs intern vs technician vs trainee authorization, vaccination age/qualification, pharmacist prescribing
- Massachusetts vs federal requirements and adverse exceptions in short scenarios

**Novelty gate:** select a new drug, prescriber role, product formulation, actor or key deciding fact; do not merely rewrite existing E30-114/E30-139/E30-331 schedule cases.

## Form F — 120, institutional workflow and compounding operations

Prioritize scenario applications not merely citation recognition:
- Sterile / hazardous / nonsterile compounding differences (USP <795>, <797>, <800>), Category 1/2/3, C-SCA and ISO classifications, BUD, engineering / personnel / cleaning / environmental monitoring
- NIOSH, OSHA, hazardous-drug dosage manipulation, storage and refrigerator controls
- Hospital/clinic bulk-controlled inventory, nursing-unit partial waste, DPH loss notifications, LTCF dispensing
- Supply chain, delivery, home/worksite/specialty, storage excursions, technology downtime, central-fill responsibilities
- Recall, adulteration, misbranding, REMS, FDA Orange/Purple Book and product selection

**Novelty gate:** assert verified effective-date and source-version before answer drafting; avoid disputed USP wording unless normative text or explicit Board source supports that precise rule.

## Form G — 120, fresh integrated judgment and final rehearsal

Use multi-step cases requiring correct authority, actor, clock and justified legal action, not repeated statute recall:
- Conflicting federal/state clocks and prescriber identities
- Board inspections/discipline/impairment, loss/theft report paths and statutory notices
- HIPAA vs Part 2 vs Massachusetts confidentiality and breach notifications
- Interacting delivery/cold-chain/controlled inventory/recall duties
- Public health vaccination and infectious-disease pharmacist pathways
- Varied unknown combinations with defensible A–D explanations and no invented permissions
- Some short direct questions to ensure law knowledge is not dependent on remembering one scenario narrative

**Novelty gate:** compare legal decision/actor/time-trigger against all 480 prior A/B/C/D and new E/F questions; maintain a source/duplicate ledger.

## Completion gates for each future batch

1. Determine precise regulation, official source URL, and date before writing.
2. Draft scenario and all four options; verify each option, exemptions and numeric deadlines with official source.
3. Check source-authentication limitations and legal holds; hold ambiguous cases, do not force a key.
4. Deduplicate by **legal decision tested** as well as literal and lexical text.
5. Ensure old exams and storage remain intact.
6. Build/verify public form only after legal review. Assess readiness principally on first-attempt *unseen* performance, not high scores on recycled mixed items.

**No E/F/G launch or law approval is claimed by this design document.**
