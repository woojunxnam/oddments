## Why 120 × 2 is appropriate, and what it cannot prove

Official [NABP MPJE](https://nabp.pharmacy/programs/examinations/mpje/take-the-mpje-exam/) is **120 questions in 2.5 hours (150 minutes)**, computer-adaptive, with one-way delivery. NABP's 2025 college webinar described **100 scored + 20 pretest items**; our mock will score all 120 because we cannot identify real psychometric pretest items. Any raw mock percentage is an **editorial study indicator**, not a NABP scaled score or validated pass probability.

The owner may have previously practiced some/all 215 items. Two disjoint 120-item forms prevent within-form/other-form exact question overlap **but do not make previously studied items unseen**. The future mock must calculate **prior exposure** from same-browser Chapter 30 attempts, and report prior-seen vs first-attempt-unseen performance separately. With only 25 new items, two forms alone cannot honestly guarantee exam readiness; the official Pre-MPJE can supply a separate benchmark.

## Two test forms — final requirements

**Form construction:** after finishing legal/novelty QA, lock a deterministic versioned 240-ID pool. Partition into 2 sets of 120 each without replacement. Freeze assignment and ordering using a manifest with bank SHA/version and difficulty/skill bands. Do not re-randomize between tests; avoid two near-identical legal concepts in the same form. A full question bank may include a repeated topic, but distinct legal micro-skills should be represented.

**2026 v 2027 blueprint gate:** [NABP announced](https://nabp.pharmacy/news/blog/mpje-content-outline/) a **March 1, 2027** change. If examination is before that date use pre-2027 Competency Statements. If on/after March 1, **retag all 240 to the NEW four domains** and rebalance two forms near **36/36/24/24** for a 120-item simulation (30/30/20/20 is the published content weighting for the 100 operational items; the scaled raw 120 approximation is our custom simulation). Do not silently relabel legacy numeric Areas to the new domains. If official target domain quotas are infeasible from approved 240, adjust future item selection **before publishing** or disclose the mismatch; never invent tag values.

**Mock UX:** single item visible at a time; 150:00 timer with reload continuation; one-way **Next**, no return; no correctness feedback while testing; one-session practice answer per item; incomplete answers are allowed to skip if explicitly confirmed, not accidentally scored correct; submit after question 120 or time expiry; explanations/sources only in post-submit review. Score all 120 with SATA exact-match and preserve question-level attempts in a form-specific namespace. Display total, time, per-domain, high-consequence legal error clusters, seen/unseen first-attempt counts, flagged uncertainty items, and source review status. Export privacy-preserving JSON/CSV summary locally for later discussion.

**Readiness rubric — editorial, NOT NABP scaled-score cut point:** Provisional 'strong practice readiness' only when BOTH untutored first attempts finish within 150 minutes, each reaches **≥85% raw** (≥102/120), each tested domain ≥80%, no unresolved wrong answers involving critical patient-safety law, and a meaningful unseen count supports inference. **75–84% or repeated high-risk misses** = review targeted law and retest fresh items. **Below 75%** = systematic re-study. If most items were previously answered in practice, label the result 'practice-bank mastery' rather than credible independent exam readiness. These percentages are pragmatic study thresholds only and have **no statistically validated link** to MPJE pass/fail. Do not promise passing from a high mock score.

**Release gate:** 240 approved legal decisions, stable/verified exact questions, 120+120 no duplicate IDs, date-specific legal/blueprint review, 2 live browser test completions, stored result audit, and user actually completes both forms.
