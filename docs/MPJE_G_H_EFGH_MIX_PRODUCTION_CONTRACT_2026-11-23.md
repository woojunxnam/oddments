# Massachusetts MPJE — Form G, Form H and E/F/G/H Mixed Exams
**Frozen production contract — November 23, 2026.** Owner's requested final scope: Forms A–H, 960 unique items. G and H are new five-option exams, 120 questions each; four EFGH mixed exams reuse the 480 E–H questions without overlap within that mixed family. No Form I planned. 

## Official scope and limitations
Use the Massachusetts-specific MPJE, **NABP legacy Competency Statements through February 28, 2027**. Official blueprint: https://nabp.pharmacy/programs/examinations/mpje/competency-statements/ ; exam format: https://nabp.pharmacy/programs/examinations/mpje/take-the-mpje-exam/ ; March 1, 2027 transition: https://nabp.pharmacy/news/blog/mpje-content-outline/ . Massachusetts license guidance: https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-pharmacist-license . Legacy areas are 22/33/24/21%; integer targets per 120-question form are 26/40/29/25. The 22 competency IDs are authoritative; our topic summaries and per-ID quotas below are editorial targets, not official NABP item counts. Current law must be checked as of each item's scenario date. These are nonadaptive, AI-authored educational assessments with raw scores, not calibrated or independently certified NABP tests.

## Roles
- G: **Final Readiness Assessment**. Representative breadth and realistic first-attempt testing. 30 direct/concise application, 66 ordinary scenario judgments, 24 integrated/exception items. Cover all 22 competency IDs; restore common high-value concepts even where E/F already covered them.
- H: **Independent Confirmation / Robustness Assessment**. A parallel 120-question exam, not a collection of obscure rules and not a harder-for-its-own-sake extension. 36 concise/direct, 60 ordinary applied, 24 integrated/exception. Test recurring core laws through materially different determining facts, including borderline lawful/unlawful choices, state/federal interaction, chronological validity, common drug schedule and prescribing decisions. Avoid reuse of G's key decisions in the same fact pattern. Do not author H based on the user's responses to G; preserve H as an unseen check.
- Both: 120 original English questions; exactly five A–E choices; sensible mix of SBA and SATA, generally about 55–65% SBA and 35–45% SATA, adjusted for natural legal content; not an official format ratio. SATA 2–4 correct where appropriate, exact match. No visible answer-count/key-pattern hints or intentional trick wording. Vary stem/choice lengths and avoid implausible distractors. Use common concepts, not artificially novel esoteric laws merely to avoid overlap.

## Content and source controls
1. Read A–F as read-only reference for same-decision collisions; read G for H. No retrospective full-bank legal audit or release hold for old A–F. Repeat a core statute when the decisive fact, role, setting, exception or required action changes materially. Do not use same stem or outcome-equivalent case with cosmetic edits. 
2. Prioritize community pharmacy workflows plus plausible hospital/LTCF/clinic and nonresident settings. Apply Massachusetts and federal provisions together where relevant. Source hierarchy: Massachusetts statutes, CMR, effective Board/DPH policies; current CFR/USC, DEA/FDA/HHS; official USP sources for applicable standards. No invented dates, mandatory restrictions or citations; replace unverifiable candidates during the same batch. Distinguish legal mandates from advisory/best-practice recommendations.
3. Each item retains the Form F schema (five explanations, source URLs, precise citation, verified_at, legal_decision, critical_exception, counterfactual_flip, comparison IDs, review status, revision), plus difficulty tier, accurate legacy primary competency, optional secondary tags, and critical-error option tags with rationale where warranted. Flag a selected illegal or dangerous action, or omitted essential mandatory safeguard, only when the specific choice warrants it; not every incorrect response is a critical error.
4. Verify per batch, save to isolated draft branch, read back and run item/key/option/source-field/ID/novelty/blueprint checks. Author-source review is not independent pharmacist or legal expert approval. At 120, one bounded form-specific final gate, fixed manifest signature, UI regression, merge and verified public Pages URL. No unbounded re-audit loop. Preserve existing A–F content and storage histories. Do not print exam items/keys in chat before blind attempt.

## Readiness and review
G and H are intended to be taken blind, without references, before taking EFGH mixes or opening their solutions. Same 150-minute timer, one-way navigation, exact SATA grading, draft resume, expiry, prior attempts, review filters, exports. First-attempt raw score and area results, competency results with small-sample caveats, critical error explanations, self-reported uncertainty/guesses, unanswered count, timing and source-linked review. Editorial study benchmark for each G/H: ≥100/120 first attempt, ≥75% in every Area, all 120 submitted in time, and no unresolved critical errors after targeted correction with a fresh independent scenario. A strong score is study evidence, not a validated pass probability. Mixed exams are retention/consistency checks, not independent readiness predictions after prior exposure. Optional user-facing confidence marker must be optional and must never affect raw grading.
