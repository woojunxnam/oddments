Circular Letter
DCP 22-07-113 – Changes to Prescription Monitoring Program (PMP) Reporting Requirements
Date:
08/17/2022
Referenced Sources:
Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 94C Section 24A
M.G.L. c. 94C section 19B
The purpose of this circular letter is to inform Massachusetts pharmacies of the release of a new version of the Data Submission Guide for Dispensers, Version 5.0.  The PMP Clearinghouse is upgrading to the ASAP 4.2A format, which contains several new data fields.  Version 5.0 provides reporting guidance for these new data fields, reporting clarifications for several existing fields, and guidance for the reporting of dispensed opioid antagonists (Naloxone) required by MGL Chapter 94C Section19B.
Pharmacies should be prepared to comply with these changes no later than October 1, 2022.
