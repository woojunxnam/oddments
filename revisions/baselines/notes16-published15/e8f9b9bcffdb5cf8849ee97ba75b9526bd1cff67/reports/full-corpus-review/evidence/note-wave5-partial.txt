          TO:                Massachusetts Controlled Substance Registrants and

          FROM:              James Lavery, Director, Bureau of Health Professions Licensure
                             David Sencabaugh, Executive Director, Board of Registration in Pharmacy

          DATE:              November 1, 2018

          RE:                Patient Requests for Partial Fill of Schedule II Prescriptions

          On August 9, 2018, Governor Baker signed into law chapter 208 of the acts of 2018, An Act for
          Prevention and Access to Appropriate Care and Treatment of Addiction. The law updates
          M.G.L. c. 94C, § 18(d¾) relative to partial fill of prescriptions for Schedule II medications. 1
          These updates allow a patient to request the remainder of the partially filled prescription within
          30 days of the prescription being issued.

          These updates to the law do not add requirements to or change the process for prescribers.
          Updated guidance on how pharmacists should process requests for partial fill of a prescription is
          included below.

                •     If requested by the patient, a pharmacist may dispense a lesser quantity than indicated on
                      any Schedule II prescription.

                •     The remaining portion of the partially filled Schedule II prescription may be filled within
                      30 days of the prescription issue date, in accordance with state and federal law.

                •     Only the pharmacy dispensing the original quantity may dispense the remaining portion.

                •     As a reminder, for Schedule II prescriptions issued by prescribers who are licensed in
                      another state and do not hold a Massachusetts Controlled Substance Registration,
                      provided they meet the requirements of M.G.L. c. 94C, § 18(d) or § 18(d½), the initial
                      partial dispensing shall be filled within 5 days of the prescription issue date, and the
                      remaining portion may be filled within 30 days of the prescription issue date.

   •   Upon an initial partial dispensing of a prescription, or a subsequent dispensing of a
       remaining portion, a notation must be made in the patient's record maintained by the
       pharmacy. This information must be accessible to the prescriber upon request.

   •   A patient requesting a partial fill for any Schedule II prescription covered under their
       insurance plan may not be charged an additional payment, including, but not limited to,
       co-payments, if filling the remaining portion of the prescription.

   •   Per M.G.L. c. 94C, § 22(c), prescribers must include a partial fill notation on all Schedule
       II opioid prescriptions. Prescribers may include this notation on Schedule II opioid
       prescriptions in writing or electronically.

          o DPH considers use of the following abbreviated phrase on all prescriptions for a
            Schedule II opioid to meet the requirements of:

              “Partial fill upon patient request”

   •   A pharmacist receiving a prescription for a Schedule II opioid without the required
       notation must contact the prescriber or prescriber’s authorized agent for confirmation and
       document the notation on the prescription in writing or electronically.

Questions or concerns regarding this information should be directed to: dcp.dph@mass.gov
