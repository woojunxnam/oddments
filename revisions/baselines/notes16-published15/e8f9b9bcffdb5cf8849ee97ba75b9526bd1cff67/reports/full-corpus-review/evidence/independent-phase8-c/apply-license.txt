What you need
Submitted NABP application on National Association of Boards of Pharmacy
Completed Massachusetts licensure application, including notarized signature
Copy of birth certificate, valid passport, or driver’s license
2”x 2” passport-sized photograph
Verification of Graduation form (first-time pharmacist applicants only)
Official report of NAPLEX score sent directly from NABP (Score Transfer applicants only)
Foreign Pharmacists
If you were educated outside the U.S., you must obtain FPGEC Certification.
Exams
You must complete the following exams:
NAPLEX (North American Pharmacist Licensure Examination)
MPJE (Multistate Pharmacy Jurisprudence Examination)
Approved applicants must contact Pearson VUE to:
Schedule the exam date.
Choose an exam location.

How to apply
Online
+
Go to the Health Professions Licensing Portal  and create an account, or log into your existing account. If this is your first time using this new portal, please visit Health Professions Licensing System User Guide.
More info
How many intern hours are required for licensure in the state of Massachusetts?
A minimum of 1500 hours.Do my application and fees expire?
Yes, the application is void if requirements are not met within one year from date of receipt. The fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
