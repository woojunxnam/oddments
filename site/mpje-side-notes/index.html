How these notes are organized

Each topic begins with a plain-English explanation, then identifies the exact Massachusetts or federal source, applies it to a pharmacy example, and ends with an exam trap. Click the original-note link to compare the Korean source.

Legal-status rule: Published statutes and regulations are different from draft regulations, Board policies, FDA guidance, and USP standards. This guide labels those distinctions.
