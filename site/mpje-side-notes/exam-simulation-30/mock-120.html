How full-length simulation works
  • Choose one form and work in order. You cannot return to a question after pressing Next.
  • SATA questions use exact-match grading. You must choose an answer before Next; questions cannot be skipped, as on the actual MPJE.
  • The 150-minute clock continues if you reload, switch tabs, or leave the page. No pause is available.
  • Answers and citations remain hidden while the exam is active. All 120 questions are counted as scored for this practice; the actual MPJE is adaptive.
  • Study exposures already recorded in this browser are snapshotted before starting. Results separate previously practiced questions from first-time unseen submitted answers.
  • The four area labels reflect the pre-2027 outline, not the new blueprint effective March 1, 2027. Avoid interpreting scores as a predicted NABP pass/fail result.
