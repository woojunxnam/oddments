What can I practice today?

Choose any chapter for 10 original scored A–D questions. Chapters 1 (Licensure / CE), 2 (Personnel / Supervision) and 21 (Nonsterile Compounding) each also have 10 additional scenarios, giving them 20 questions. New Chapters 1 and 2 scenarios include an official legal citation with each answer. Select a choice, check the answer, read explanations for A–D, and reset to retry.

Why the count changed: An earlier version advertised 540 questions by mechanically converting each source question's incorrect-answer explanations into a second question. That led to misleading prompts, incomplete fragments used as answer choices, and distractors that were not reliable. Those 260 auto-generated drills were withdrawn rather than presented as legal exam preparation. Twenty replacements have now been individually written and checked, while 240 remain to be written and reviewed as genuine scenarios with one best answer and three explained distractors.

Legal accuracy boundary: The first twenty replacement scenarios were checked against Massachusetts 247 CMR 3, 4 and 8 and the Board’s 2026 personnel and 2025-revised CE policies. The original 270 passed a four-choice/answer-key structural check, not an independent legal audit for every question. Some original guides discuss unresolved policy/regulation conflicts. See each guide’s source links before memorizing disputed rules.
