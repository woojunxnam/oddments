How this cumulative bank is maintained

Collection: Each chapter has ten pre-existing English multiple-choice questions. This page reads that chapter's published HTML and reproduces its questions with the original answer explanations. Supplement: Each chapter has ten further questions. For 26 chapters these are error-diagnosis exercises built from the original question's A–D explanations; the nonsterile compounding chapter has ten separately authored application scenarios. This is 27 × (10 + 10) = 540 practice questions.

Why load one chapter at a time? A 540-item page would be unwieldy on mobile. The selector provides access to the whole combined bank while loading only twenty questions for the chosen chapter. Use the chapter's linked guide for legal citations and to resolve topics requiring current re-verification. Existing original notes, chapter quizzes, and question banks were not edited.

Legal uncertainty: Not every answer in a large cumulative collection has received a new independent case-by-case legal audit on November 10, 2026. Prior chapter reviews identify official sources and unresolved regulatory conflicts, and the Open Register tracks these boundaries. New or later-dated Board, DPH, DEA, FDA, USP and NABP requirements may change the correct answer.
