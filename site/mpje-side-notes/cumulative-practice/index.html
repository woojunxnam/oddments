Why the bank is growing: An older version mechanically converted existing answer rationales into unreliable new questions. Those synthetic items were withdrawn and replaced with individually written cases. This new difficulty expansion likewise uses original cases written against official law, not automatic paraphrases. The 180 new items underwent preliminary author/source and structural checks; independent legal auditing and the other 360 cases remain outstanding.

Legal accuracy boundary: The 180 new Moderate and Hard scenarios were authored using applicable federal and Massachusetts authorities and checked for correct A–D structure, explanations, and citations; comprehensive independent legal auditing has not been completed. Existing 540 questions were preserved, and previous structural validation is not a substitute for legal review. Where Board policies and laws conflict, consult the linked official text before memorizing a rule.
