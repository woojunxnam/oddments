What can I practice today?

Every chapter has 10 original scored A–D questions + 10 supplemental scenarios + 10 Moderate + 10 Hard questions. Newly completed Chapters 1–4 cover licensure/CE, personnel, pharmacy/MOR and controlled schedules; Chapters 26–27 cover brand/generic/schedules and legal-source conflicts. Select a choice and check your answer to reveal explanations of all four options and a link to the controlling official legal source.

Why the bank is growing: An older version mechanically converted existing answer rationales into unreliable new questions. Those synthetic items were withdrawn and replaced with individually written cases. This new difficulty expansion likewise uses original cases written against official law, not automatic paraphrases. All 540 difficulty-tier questions were individually authored and underwent preliminary source and structural checks; independent substantive legal auditing and production browser validation are separate requirements.

Legal accuracy boundary: The 540 new Moderate and Hard scenarios were authored using applicable federal and Massachusetts authorities and checked for correct A–D structure, explanations, and citations; comprehensive independent legal auditing has not been completed. Existing 540 questions were preserved, and previous structural validation is not a substitute for legal review. Where Board policies and laws conflict, consult the linked official text before memorizing a rule.
