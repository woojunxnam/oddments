High-yield MPJE memory rules

Federal Schedule II: no refills; Massachusetts out-of-state narcotic and nonnarcotic prescription pathways differ. Schedules III–V: federal refill and transfer rules matter. Massachusetts Schedule VI: not a sixth federal controlled-substance schedule; most routine prescription drugs fall here. Product matters: the same ingredient can appear in different brands, formulations, strengths, approved indications, OTC/Rx statuses or REMS programs. Substitution: shared generic ingredient alone does not establish lawful therapeutic or biological interchangeability.

Verification boundaries: this lab reuses Chapter 26's 127 catalog entries and adds 24 educational examples. Individual NDCs, active brand marketing, FDA labeling revisions and all product-specific exceptions have not been independently audited. Check current DailyMed, DEA schedules, 21 CFR Part 1308, 247 CMR 9, Orange Book and Purple Book for product-specific decisions.
