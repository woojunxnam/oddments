Massachusetts Department of Public Health
Statewide Guidance for Pharmacist
s
 to Prescribe Prophylaxis 
after 
Exposure to Certain Sexually Transmitted Infections 
Amendments to
 
M.G.L. c.94C s19F
 
allow pharmacists to prescribe prophylaxis for sexually transmitted infections (STIs). The Massachusetts Department of Public Health (DPH) currently recommends doxycycline prophylaxis for STIs by pharmacists 
if the following conditions are met:  
Pharmacists:
Have received 
training in 
accordance with 
Circular Letter
: 
DCP 26-08-125
 
Pharmacist prescribing of certain contraceptives and medications for the prevention of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) and prevention and treatment of sexually transmitted infections (STI)
.
 
Prescribe prevention treatment for STIs according to
 
Massachusetts Department of Public Health Prevention Guidelines
.
Provide information about
 
Massachusetts Integrated Testing and Linkage Services sites
 
or
 
Sexual and Reproductive Health Services locations
 
for additional questions, follow-up testing, follow-up care and care of sex partners.
Ask patient if they have a primary care 
provider
 they would like to have notified. If yes, obtain verbal consent before notification.
If the patient does not have a primary care provider, provide a list of primary care providers in the surrounding geographic area.  
Patients:
Provide result/evidence of negative laboratory testing within the past week showing absence of chlamydia, gonorrhea, and syphilis. 
Provide result/evidence of a negative pregnancy test within the past week (for individuals capable of pregnancy).
Sign 
waiver
 indicating 
individual
 is asymptomatic, and declines physical examination, 
in spite of
 awareness that an examination may change treatment recommendations.
Are 18 years of age or older.
Weigh more than 45 kg (99 lbs).
