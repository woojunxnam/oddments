Regulation&nbsp;
                247 CMR 8.00: Pharmacy interns and technicians
Date:
04/25/2025
Organization:
Board of Registration in Pharmacy
Regulatory Authority:
MGL c. 94C, § 6 
MGL c. 112, §§ 24C, 24D, 24E, 24F, 24G and 42A
Official Version:
Purchase the official print version
247 CMR 8.00 sets forth eligibility requirements and procedures to obtain licensure as a pharmacy intern, a pharmacy technician or a pharmacy technician trainee. It also sets forth scope of practice for each of these licensure types.
