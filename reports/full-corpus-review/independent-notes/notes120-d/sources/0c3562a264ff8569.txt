§ 266.505 Prohibition on sewering hazardous waste pharmaceuticals.
All healthcare facilities—including very small quantity generators operating under
§ 262.14 of this subchapter
in lieu of this subpart—and reverse distributors are prohibited from discharging hazardous waste pharmaceuticals to a sewer system that passes through to a publicly-owned treatment works. Healthcare facilities and reverse distributors remain subject to the prohibitions in
40 CFR 403.5(b)
.
[
88 FR 54113
, Aug. 9, 2023]
