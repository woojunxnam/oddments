Biosimilars are safe and effective treatment options for many illnesses such as chronic skin and bowel diseases (like psoriasis, irritable bowel syndrome, Crohn’s disease and colitis), arthritis, kidney conditions, and cancer. Biosimilars increase access to lifesaving medications at potentially lower costs. Explore the website to learn more about biosimilars and to access all FDA’s educational resources for health care providers and patients.
