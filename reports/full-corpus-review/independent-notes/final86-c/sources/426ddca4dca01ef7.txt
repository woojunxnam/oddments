                                                   MEMORANDUM


   TO: All Board-Licensed Retail Pharmacies

   FROM: Massachusetts Board of Registration in Pharmacy

   DATE: October 13, 2022

   SUBJECT: Promethazine with Codeine Products


   The Board is aware that promethazine with codeine products have become highly sought-after
   products for illicit use. This has resulted in fraudulent prescriptions and pharmacy robberies
   across the country.

   Since this creates a safety issue for both pharmacy staff and the public, several pharmacies
   have requested to waive the requirement to stock these products. 247 CMR 6.02(4) requires
   pharmacies to maintain a sufficient variety of medications to meet the needs of the
   community.

   Due to the current situation, the Board does not intend to enforce the regulation to
   stock promethazine with codeine products making waivers unnecessary.

   However, if a valid prescription is received, the pharmacist must contact the prescriber to find
   an alternative product or arrange for the medication to be provided by another means (e.g.,
   mail order, central fill, etc.)

   In order to deter thefts, the Board has no objection to the posting of signage stating that these
   products are not available.

   The Board will continue to monitor the situation over the next year and will reassess at that
   time.
