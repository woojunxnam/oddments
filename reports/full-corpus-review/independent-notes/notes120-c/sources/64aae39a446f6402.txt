How to submit
Online
+
File a Drug Incident Report using our
Health Professions Licensing System
.
Next steps
Intake number
When you file a Drug Incident Report an Incident report # is assigned and appears in the top left corner of the report.  Please refer to this Incident report # in any communication with DCP related to this incident and submit any additional relevant information to
DIR.DCP@mass.gov
.
More info
The submission of the DIR form will satisfy DCP's requirements for both a telephonic and written report (the latter is currently required within 7 days of discovery).
