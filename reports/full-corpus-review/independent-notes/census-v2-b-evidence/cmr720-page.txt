Regulation
Regulation
105 CMR 720.00: List of interchangeable drug products
Date:
07/09/2004
Organization:
Department of Public Health
Regulatory Authority:
MGL c. 17, § 13
MGL c. 112, § 12D
Official Version:
Purchase the official print version
105 CMR 720.000 establishes a drug formulary, or list of interchangeable drug products, for use by physicians, other practitioners, and pharmacists licensed to practice within the commonwealth, so that consumers of prescription drug products may realize cost savings by buying less expensive, safe drug products.
