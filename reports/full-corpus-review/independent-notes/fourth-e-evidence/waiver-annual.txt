Massachusetts Request for Annual Waiver of Daily Data Submission
             In Accordance with the Controlled Substances Act, M.G.L. Chapter 94C
Pharmacies that do not dispense Controlled Substances in Schedules II-V, or any additional drugs that the Department has determined must be reported to the PMP, may complete this form to request a waiver of the requirements that pharmacies must report to the PMP. If a pharmacy receiving this waiver nevertheless dispenses from Schedules II-V or any additional drug required for submission to the PMP by the Department, the waiver will not apply to such activity. The pharmacy will be required to report such dispensing to the PMP. Please submit to the Department by July 1st of each year via email to: mapmp.dph@mass.gov
